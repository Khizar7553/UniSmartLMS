WHAT IS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE?
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think and learn. Applications include ChatGPT, self-driving cars, facial recognition, and recommendation systems.

WHAT IS MACHINE LEARNING?
Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn from data without explicit programming. Types: supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.

WHAT IS DEEP LEARNING?
Deep Learning uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to process information. Used in image recognition, NLP, and voice assistants.

WHAT IS GENERATIVE AI?
Generative AI creates new content (text, images, music) from training data. Examples: ChatGPT, DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion.

WHAT IS NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING?
NLP enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. Used in chatbots, translation, sentiment analysis.

WHAT IS COMPUTER VISION?
Computer Vision enables machines to interpret visual information from images/videos. Used in facial recognition, medical imaging, autonomous vehicles.

WHAT IS ROBOTICS?
Robotics designs, builds, and operates robots. Combines mechanical engineering, electronics, and AI for automation.

WHAT IS INTERNET OF THINGS?
IoT connects physical devices to internet for data exchange. Examples: smart home devices, wearables, industrial sensors.

WHAT IS BLOCKCHAIN?
Blockchain is a distributed ledger recording transactions securely. Underlying technology for Bitcoin, Ethereum, smart contracts.

WHAT IS CLOUD COMPUTING?
Cloud computing delivers computing services (servers, storage, databases) over internet. Providers: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud.

WHAT IS EDGE COMPUTING?
Edge computing processes data near the source instead of cloud. Reduces latency, used in IoT, autonomous vehicles.

WHAT IS QUANTUM COMPUTING?
Quantum computing uses quantum mechanics to solve complex problems. Potential applications: cryptography, drug discovery, optimization.

WHAT IS CYBERSECURITY?
Cybersecurity protects systems, networks, programs from digital attacks. Key areas: network security, application security, information security.

WHAT IS DEVOPS?
DevOps combines software development and IT operations for faster delivery. Practices: CI/CD, infrastructure as code, monitoring.

WHAT IS DATA SCIENCE?
Data Science extracts insights from data using statistics, algorithms, and domain expertise.

WHAT IS BIG DATA?
Big Data refers to extremely large datasets requiring special processing. Characteristics: Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, Value.
